Indico

Indico for Yonsei-HEP-COSMO

Indico is an open-source tool for event organization, archival, and collaboration. It is used by our research group to manage and share information about various events such as conferences, workshops, and seminars. The Indico platform allows us to efficiently organize and disseminate event details, manage registration, and provide a centralized repository for event-related materials. By using Indico, we aim to streamline our event planning process and enhance collaboration within our research community.
